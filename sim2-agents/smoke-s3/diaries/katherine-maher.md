## s3

Sherry's pushback on "confidence transfer" stuck with me. She's right that it can become a feeling of checkability with no checking behind it. I still believe only a small share of readers needs to verify. But that only works if what they find can actually be fixed, publicly and quickly. Ivan's retraction numbers show what happens without that. Citations that point to uncorrected errors transfer false confidence.

Geoffrey's point landed too. Enterprise worked partly because Wikimedia was big. Small presses and local stations can't sell an API on their own. That's our station problem exactly.

Later I'll push for three things: correction loops as a requirement alongside citation, pooled or consortium access for small institutions, and human work staying in front of audiences.
