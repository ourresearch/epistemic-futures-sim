I'm a historian of piracy and of how print became credible, so I'll offer the long view and not claim more than it can bear. I've never worked on AI training and copyright as such.

### 34. Alex Springer (round2)

Adrian asked who guards the guards, and I'll give an honest answer from the standard I maintain. Retrieval can be checked by two independent witnesses. Nick, I'm fine with coarse payouts. The records underneath them should still be detailed.
