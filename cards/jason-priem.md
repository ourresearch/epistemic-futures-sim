# Position card: Jason Priem

## 1. Role at summit and bio

**Role:** Panelist in **Session 3: "Knowledge Infrastructure and Tools: Commons, Enclosure, and Sovereignty"**, with Adrian Johns (likely lead), Adam Bly, Benjamin Bratton and David Weinberger. He is the one person in that session who operates large-scale open scholarly infrastructure day to day.

**Bio:** Priem is co-founder and CEO of OpenAlex, the nonprofit formerly called OurResearch and before that Impactstory. He co-built Unpaywall, Unsub and OpenAlex, the CC0 index of about 480M scholarly works. He co-coined "altmetrics" and lead-authored the 2010 Altmetrics Manifesto. His long-running argument is that journals and peer review are "patches on a fundamentally broken" system that will be replaced by open, algorithmic, post-publication filtering. Since 2025 he has presented himself as an **"AI evangelist."** OpenAlex is being rebuilt around AI-assisted engineering, agent-legible APIs and documentation, and a "post-GUI" strategy. OurResearch formally adopted POSI in 2021.

## 3. Relation to the concept note

**Endorses:**

## 4. Predicted contributions

**Session 3 harvest claims:**
1. **Open data is the sovereignty move.** If the scholarly record is open and machine-readable, nobody has to go "with your hat in your hand" to whoever controls data and models. Intelligence becomes swappable, including open-weight models.
2. **The commons needs a sustainability model for the post-GUI era:** free data, metered paid services. Otherwise commercial actors will own and norm it.
3. **Infrastructure must be agent-legible and comprehensive** (a "Library of Alexandria"), including non-English and Global South literature (the IRDB/Japan ingestion and the Anglosphere point, `av/2026--…-q2-2026….md`).
4. **Trust through open verification.** LLM outputs should link to open records so every claim is checkable. Open data enables open forensics on paper mills and citation manipulation.

**Session 5 interventions:**
- **Fund open, CC0, agent-ready scholarly metadata (and OA full text) as core public infrastructure**, with POSI guarantees and usage-based service revenue.
- **Open community curation:** let institutions, authors and funders directly correct the shared record, humans in the loop as curators of an open commons.
- **Reproducible, shared prompts and open tooling** as a norm for AI-assisted scholarship and analytics, which he explicitly analogizes to open source.
